Cocktail Hour

A humorous tycoon game: run your parents’ cocktail bar on a space station.

Summary

Genre
2D Tycoon
My Role
Team Lead · Lead Game Designer
Team Size
5
Duration
Sep – Nov 2023 (3 months)
Engine
Pygame
Language
Python

In the year 5033, space travel is part of everyday life. You come home to Moonshine Station, but your parents leave for a space trip and ask you to run their small cocktail bar. Serve weary travelers from different planets and keep the bar alive.

Gameplay video

What I Did

Systems & Tycoon Design

  • Did all of the design work except worldbuilding and narrative.
  • Designed the data model behind the core loop: customer taste parameters and cocktail ingredient attributes.
  • Co-designed the customer rating system with the programmer, and defined all tycoon rules and mechanics.

Team Lead

  • Led a team of five, most of whom were making a game for the first time.
  • Bridged designers, programmers, and artists, for example explaining pixels per block and sprite sheets to the art team.

Postmortem

✅ What went well

  • We completed the game and showed it at an exhibition.

💡 What I learned

  • Team structure matters: we had too many artists for the scope.
  • How to communicate across roles as a designer.

🔧 What I'd improve

  • The scope was too big for one semester and a mostly first-time team.
  • The UI, and how well the game conveyed our world and its immersion.
