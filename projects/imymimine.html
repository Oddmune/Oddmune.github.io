IMYMIMINE

An invitation from a parallel universe: meet another version of yourself.

Summary

Genre

Team Size

Duration

Players meet another version of themselves in a parallel universe, and face different endings based on their choices. Inspired by the film Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022).

Gameplay video

What I Did

Programming (Unity / C#)

  • Programmed the entire game as the sole programmer, learning C# along the way.
  • Built the scene transition and dialogue systems, using Yarn Spinner for dialogue, and integrated all artist assets.
  • Split scenes around player choices so each scene only loads the resources it needs.

Narrative & Team Lead

  • Wrote the storyboard and managed the team schedule.
  • Found a shared art direction so the 3D backgrounds and 2D characters blend together.
