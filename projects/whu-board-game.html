What I Did

Board & Level Design

  • Solely designed the board for balanced, strategic gameplay, and drew the entire board.

Graphic Design & QA

  • Designed the card and rulebook layouts, the logo, and the introductory comic.
  • Playtested the game to find holes in the rules.

Postmortem

✅ What went well

  • We crowdfunded and manufactured a real board game.

💡 What I learned

  • Why a project needs a clear leader, and what a leader should do for good teamwork.

🔧 What I'd improve

  • Share workload more visibly: a lot of design work landed on me without the team knowing.
  • Meeting the price point while keeping the quality high.
